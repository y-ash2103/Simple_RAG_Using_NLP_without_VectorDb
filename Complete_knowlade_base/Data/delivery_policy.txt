iStore Hub Delivery Policy

1. Standard delivery is available to supported serviceable locations.
2. Estimated delivery time depends on location, stock availability and courier operations.
3. Customers receive an order or tracking reference after dispatch when tracking is available.
4. Delivery estimates are not guaranteed delivery dates.
5. Customers should inspect the package for visible damage at delivery.
6. If the package appears severely damaged or tampered with, the customer should document the condition and contact support.
7. Delivery charges, if applicable, are displayed during checkout.
