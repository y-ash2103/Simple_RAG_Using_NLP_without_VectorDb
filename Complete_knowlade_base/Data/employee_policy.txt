iStore Hub Employee Policy

1. Employees must provide accurate information and must not knowingly misrepresent product specifications or company policies.
2. Employees must verify stock and price information before making commitments to customers.
3. Employee discounts must not be transferred to unauthorized people.
4. Customer data must be handled confidentially.
5. Employees must escalate disputes, suspected fraud, damaged products and unusual refund requests to a manager.
6. Employees must not promise a refund, exchange or warranty outcome before the required inspection or approval.
7. Employees must record relevant customer-service actions in the approved company system.
