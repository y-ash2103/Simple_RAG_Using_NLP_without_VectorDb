iStore Hub Customer Privacy Policy

1. Customer information should be collected only for legitimate business purposes.
2. Staff must not share customer phone numbers, addresses, order details or identification documents with unauthorized people.
3. Customer information should be accessed only when necessary for the assigned business task.
4. Staff must not copy customer personal information to personal devices.
5. Customers should be informed when information is required for an order, service or support process.
6. Suspected privacy incidents should be reported to the store manager or designated privacy contact.
