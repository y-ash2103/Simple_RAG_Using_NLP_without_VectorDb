iStore Hub Payment Policy

Accepted payment methods may include:
- Credit cards
- Debit cards
- UPI
- Net banking
- Approved digital wallets
- Eligible EMI options

Payment rules:
1. The order is confirmed only after successful payment authorization where applicable.
2. EMI availability depends on the customer's bank, card and eligibility.
3. Any EMI terms shown at checkout should be verified before purchase.
4. Cash payment may be accepted only at participating physical stores.
5. Customers should never share their card PIN, CVV or one-time password with store staff.
