
CANCELLATION_POLICY


iStore Hub Order Cancellation Policy

1. Customers may request cancellation before an order has been dispatched.
2. Once an order has been dispatched, cancellation may no longer be possible through the normal cancellation process.
3. A cancellation request should include the order number and customer contact details.
4. If payment was already captured and cancellation is approved, the refund is initiated according to the payment provider's processing timeline.
5. Customized or specially ordered products may have additional cancellation restrictions.

EXCHANGE_POLICY


iStore Hub Exchange Policy

1. Exchange requests must normally be made within 10 calendar days of purchase.
2. The product must be in acceptable physical condition and accompanied by the original invoice.
3. Exchange is subject to product availability.
4. A customer may exchange for another eligible model or storage configuration by paying any applicable price difference.
5. If the replacement product is cheaper, the difference is handled according to the store's refund procedure.
6. Devices with physical damage caused by the customer may not qualify for exchange.
7. Exchange approval requires inspection by store staff.
8. Once an exchange is completed, the original product cannot be returned under the same transaction.


FAQ


iStore Hub Customer FAQ

Q: Can I return an unopened iPhone after 5 days?
A: A return request may be made within 7 calendar days if the product is unused, undamaged, in original packaging and the customer has valid proof of purchase.

Q: Can I return an opened iPhone?
A: A normal change-of-mind return generally requires the product to be unused. An opened product may be considered if there is a verified manufacturing defect and the service team approves the return.

Q: Can I exchange my iPhone for a different storage size?
A: An eligible exchange may be possible within 10 calendar days, subject to inspection, availability and payment of any applicable price difference.

Q: Does the warranty cover a cracked screen?
A: Accidental or impact-related screen damage is not covered by the standard warranty described in this dataset.

Q: Can I cancel an order after it has shipped?
A: Normal cancellation is generally available only before dispatch. Contact support to check the specific order status.

Q: How long does a refund take?
A: After return approval, refund processing can take 5 to 7 business days.

Q: Can I trade in my old phone?
A: Eligible devices may be accepted for trade-in after inspection. Final value depends on model, storage, age and condition.

Q: What should I do if my delivered package is damaged?
A: Document the damage and contact iStore Hub support as soon as possible.

Q: Can staff tell me an exact current stock count?
A: Only verified inventory information should be used. Staff should check the current inventory system before confirming stock.

Q: Should I give my OTP to store staff?
A: No. Customers should never share payment OTPs with staff.

RETURN_POLICY


iStore Hub Return Policy

1. Customers may request a return within 7 calendar days of purchase.
2. The product must be unused, undamaged and in its original retail packaging.
3. The original invoice or valid proof of purchase is required.
4. The serial number or IMEI must match the purchase record.
5. Products showing physical damage, liquid damage or unauthorized modification are not eligible for normal returns.
6. An opened product may be accepted only if the product has a verified manufacturing defect and the service team approves the return.
7. Personalized or engraved products are not eligible for change-of-mind returns.
8. Refunds are normally processed after the returned product passes inspection.
9. Refund processing can take 5 to 7 business days after approval.
10. Accessories included with a returned product should also be returned where applicable.

Example:
A customer who bought an iPhone 17 five days ago and has not opened the box may request a return, provided all other conditions are satisfied.

SUPPORT_ESCALATION


iStore Hub Support Escalation Rules

Escalate to a manager or designated support team when:
- A customer disputes a refund decision.
- A device has suspected liquid or physical damage.
- A customer reports suspected fraud.
- A payment was deducted but the order was not created.
- A customer reports a privacy or data-security incident.
- A product's serial number does not match the purchase record.
- A customer requests an exception to company policy.
- Staff are unsure whether a warranty or return condition is satisfied.

The AI assistant should not invent an exception. It should explain the documented policy and recommend escalation when the policy requires human approval.


TRADE_IN_POLICY


iStore Hub Trade-In Policy

1. Eligible customers may exchange an old device for a trade-in value toward a new purchase.
2. Trade-in value depends on device model, storage, age, condition and inspection results.
3. The device must pass the store's inspection process.
4. The customer should remove personal data and disable account/device locks before handing over the device.
5. The final trade-in value can differ from an initial estimate after physical inspection.
6. Devices reported as lost or stolen are not eligible.
7. Required documents and eligibility checks may vary by transaction.


WARRANTY_POLICY


iStore Hub Warranty Policy

1. New iPhones sold by iStore Hub include the manufacturer's standard limited warranty applicable to the product.
2. The warranty is intended for manufacturing defects and hardware failures covered by the applicable warranty terms.
3. Accidental damage, cracked screens caused by impact, liquid damage and unauthorized repairs are not covered by the standard warranty.
4. Customers should provide proof of purchase when requesting warranty assistance.
5. Warranty eligibility may be verified using the device serial number.
6. Customers should not attempt unauthorized repairs before warranty inspection.
7. Warranty service may involve inspection, repair or replacement according to the applicable service process.
8. Warranty coverage and duration can vary by product and manufacturer terms; staff should verify the device-specific coverage before making a final commitment.
