APPLE KNOWLEDGE BASE

PRODUCT INFORMATION

iPhone is a smartphone product made by Apple.
iPhone devices are available in different models, storage capacities, and colors.
iPhone models may support features such as Face ID, wireless charging, and 5G depending on the model.
iPhone storage options vary by model.
iPhone battery life varies depending on the model, usage, settings, and network conditions.

Mac is Apple's line of personal computers.
Mac products include MacBook Air, MacBook Pro, iMac, Mac mini, and Mac Studio.
MacBook Air is designed as a lightweight laptop for everyday computing.
MacBook Pro is designed for users who need higher performance for demanding tasks.
Mac products use Apple-designed processors in supported models.

iPad is Apple's tablet product.
iPad models are available in different screen sizes and storage capacities.
Some iPad models support Apple Pencil.
Some iPad models support cellular connectivity.

Apple Watch is Apple's smartwatch product.
Apple Watch can provide fitness, health, communication, and notification features.
Apple Watch models differ in features, materials, and available connectivity.

AirPods are Apple's wireless headphones and earbuds.
Different AirPods models provide different features such as noise cancellation and spatial audio.


RETURN POLICY

Apple products purchased directly from Apple may be eligible for return according to Apple's applicable return policy.
Eligible products can generally be returned within 14 days of purchase.
The product should be in acceptable condition for return.
Proof of purchase may be required for a return.
Refunds are generally issued to the original payment method after the returned product is processed.
Return eligibility may depend on the product, purchase method, and applicable local rules.
Customized products may have different return conditions.
Products purchased from an authorized reseller are subject to the reseller's return policy.


WARRANTY POLICY

Apple products may include a limited warranty.
The standard limited warranty generally covers manufacturing defects.
The warranty does not normally cover accidental damage.
The warranty does not normally cover damage caused by unauthorized modifications.
Warranty coverage may vary depending on the product and country.
Proof of purchase may be required when requesting warranty service.


IPHONE SERVICE

Customers can request service for eligible iPhone devices.
Apple may provide hardware service for problems covered by applicable warranty or service terms.
Accidental damage may not be covered by the standard limited warranty.
Customers may be required to provide proof of purchase or device information.
Service options may include Apple Store service, authorized service providers, or other available service options.


APPLECARE

AppleCare is Apple's additional service and support program.
AppleCare plans can provide additional technical support and service coverage.
Coverage and benefits depend on the specific AppleCare plan.
AppleCare may provide coverage for certain accidental damage depending on the plan.
Customers should check the terms of their specific AppleCare plan for coverage details.


PAYMENT

Apple accepts various payment methods depending on the country and purchase channel.
Available payment methods may include credit cards and debit cards.
Payment options may vary by Apple Store, Apple Online Store, and country.
Customers should confirm available payment methods during checkout.


DELIVERY

Delivery options depend on the customer's location and the product purchased.
Estimated delivery dates may vary depending on product availability and location.
Some products may be available for pickup from an Apple Store.
Customers may receive tracking information for eligible shipments.


ORDER CANCELLATION

Customers may be able to cancel an order before it reaches a stage where cancellation is no longer possible.
Order cancellation availability depends on the order status.
If an order cannot be cancelled, the customer may need to use the applicable return process after receiving the product.


CUSTOMER SUPPORT

Apple provides technical and product support to customers.
Customers can contact Apple Support for assistance with Apple products.
Customers may receive help with product setup, troubleshooting, software issues, and service options.
